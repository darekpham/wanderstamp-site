Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · Applies to the Wanderstamp app for Android

The short version

  • Wanderstamp has no accounts and no server. Your stamps, cities, wishlist, notes and name are saved only on your phone.
  • We don't collect, see, sell or share your data. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking in the app.
  • Photos of places load from Wikimedia Commons, so Wikimedia sees the requests for those pictures.
  • Wanderstamp Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card details.

Who we are

Wanderstamp is a travel tracker app made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). If you have a question about this policy, email contact@example.com.

What the app saves, and where

Everything you put into Wanderstamp is stored in the app's own storage on your phone:

  • the countries you've stamped as visited or wanted, and the year of a visit if you give one;
  • the cities you've marked as visited;
  • your wishlist of places, cities and sights;
  • your notes on countries;
  • the name you type for your passport card (optional);
  • your settings: map theme, stamp style, card design, whether you've seen the welcome screens, and whether Pro is unlocked.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. There is no sign-in and no cloud sync. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted from your phone, and we cannot restore it because we never had a copy.

Backups

From the Profile page you can save a backup of your passport as a file and choose where it goes (for example Google Drive, email or your phone's files), and load such a file again later. A backup holds the items listed above, except your Pro status. It goes only where you send it, and the service you choose handles it under its own privacy policy.

Photos of places

City, sight and attraction pages show a photo from Wikimedia Commons. To show it, your phone downloads the picture from Wikimedia's servers. For a place without a photo chosen in advance, the app looks the place up by name on Wikipedia, and when you open a photo's credit it asks Wikimedia Commons for the author and licence.

These requests carry only what is needed to fetch the picture: the place or file name, an app name ("Wanderstamp") that Wikimedia asks apps to send, and, as with any internet request, your phone's IP address. Nothing from your passport (your stamps, notes or name) is sent. Wikimedia handles these requests under the Wikimedia Foundation privacy policy. Downloaded pictures are kept in the app's image cache on your phone so places you've opened also work offline.

Sharing and saving your passport card

When you share a passport card, Android's share menu opens and you pick the app it goes to. When you save a card to your photos, Android asks you for permission to add pictures to your photo library. The app only adds the card; it does not read or upload your photos.

Wanderstamp Pro

Pro is an optional one-time purchase (€4.99, or the equivalent shown in your currency) that unlocks extra stamp styles, card designs, map themes and travel stats. It is bought through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment and your payment details under the Google privacy policy; we do not receive your card details. Google Play tells the app whether you own Pro, and the app remembers that on your phone. We may receive the standard order reports Google Play gives developers (such as the date, country and amount of a purchase), which we use only to manage sales, refunds and taxes.

Feedback emails

"Send feedback" in the app opens your own email app with a new message. It fills in the app version and your Android version so we can look into problems; you can remove them before sending. If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply, and delete them when they're no longer needed.

Links to other places

Some buttons open other apps or websites, such as the Play Store page to rate the app or a photo's source page on Wikimedia Commons. Those services have their own privacy policies.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
  • No location access: you choose the places on the map yourself.
  • No access to your contacts, camera or microphone.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with anyone.

Children

Wanderstamp does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because your data stays on your phone, you are in full control of it: you can change or delete anything in the app, export it as a backup, or remove it all by uninstalling the app. Under laws such as the GDPR you can also ask us about any personal data we hold about you (in practice, only emails you've sent us) by writing to the address above.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what data leaves your phone, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date at the top.
