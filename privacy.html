Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026 · Applies to the Wanderstamp app for Android

The short version

  • You can use all of Wanderstamp without an account. As a guest, your stamps, cities, wishlist, notes and name are saved only on your phone, and we collect nothing about you.
  • Signing in with Google is optional. It turns on Auto backup: your passport is kept in our database in Frankfurt (EU) and synced between your phones. We use it for nothing else.
  • No ads and no tracking. The app sends anonymous usage statistics (such as "a card was shared") that can't identify you. We never sell or share your data.
  • You can delete your account and its cloud copy at any time in the app (Profile → Delete account) or by email.
  • Photos of places load from Wikimedia Commons. Wanderstamp Pro is bought through Google Play; we never see your payment details.

Who we are

Wanderstamp is made by DTech Anh Pham, a sole proprietorship registered in Poland ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described here. For anything about your data or this policy, email dndtech.biz@gmail.com.

Using Wanderstamp as a guest

Without signing in, everything you put into Wanderstamp is stored in the app's own storage on your phone:

  • the countries you've stamped as visited or wanted, and the year of a visit if you give one;
  • the cities you've marked as visited;
  • your wishlist of places, cities and sights;
  • your notes on countries;
  • the name you type for your passport card (optional);
  • your settings: map theme, stamp style, card design, whether you've seen the welcome screens, and whether Pro is unlocked.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else, and the app doesn't connect to our database at all. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted from your phone, and we can't restore it because we never had a copy.

Signing in and Auto backup

Signing in is optional and works with Sign in with Google only. When you sign in, Google confirms your account to the app, and our backend provider creates an account for you. From then on, Auto backup keeps a copy of your passport in the cloud and the same passport on every phone you sign in on.

What we store for a signed-in account:

  • your account: a user ID, the email address of your Google account and your Google profile name;
  • your passport: the countries you've stamped (and the years), your cities, your wishlist, your notes and the name on your passport card;
  • when the passport was last changed, so your phones can tell which copy is newest.

Your app settings stay on each phone, and your Pro status is not stored in the cloud: Google Play is the record of who bought Pro.

Why, and on what legal basis. We use this data only to provide the backup and sync you turned on by signing in: to keep your passport safe, restore it on a new phone and keep your phones in step. The legal basis is the performance of our agreement with you (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). We don't use it for advertising, profiling or analytics, and we don't sell or share it.

Where it is kept. The data is stored with Supabase in its Frankfurt data centre (AWS eu-central-1, Germany). Supabase processes it on our behalf, as our processor, under its data processing agreement, and may not use it for its own purposes. Each passport can be read only by the signed-in account it belongs to. All traffic between the app and Supabase is encrypted (HTTPS). Like any online service, Supabase keeps short-lived technical logs (such as sign-in times and IP addresses) for security and to run the service.

How long. We keep your account and its cloud passport until you delete the account. Deleting it removes them from our database at once; copies in the provider's routine backups disappear as those backups are replaced.

Signing out. When you sign out, the app asks whether to keep your passport on this phone or remove it from the phone. Either way, the cloud copy stays with your account for your next sign-in until you delete the account.

Google. Sign in with Google is provided by Google under the Google privacy policy. Google tells us your email address and profile name; we don't receive your Google password or access anything else in your Google account.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → Delete account. This deletes your account (user ID, email address, profile name) and the cloud copy of your passport immediately. The passport on your phone stays, so you can keep using the app as a guest. If you no longer have the app, you can ask us by email instead; see Delete your Wanderstamp account.

Backup files

From the Profile page you can also save a backup of your passport as a file and choose where it goes (for example Google Drive, email or your phone's files), and load such a file again later. A backup holds your passport and settings, except your Pro status. It goes only where you send it, and the service you choose handles it under its own privacy policy.

Photos of places

City, sight and attraction pages show a photo from Wikimedia Commons. To show it, your phone downloads the picture from Wikimedia's servers. For a place without a photo chosen in advance, the app looks the place up by name on Wikipedia, and when you open a photo's credit it asks Wikimedia Commons for the author and licence.

These requests carry only what is needed to fetch the picture: the place or file name, an app name ("Wanderstamp") that Wikimedia asks apps to send, and, as with any internet request, your phone's IP address. Nothing from your passport or account is sent. Wikimedia handles these requests under the Wikimedia Foundation privacy policy. Downloaded pictures are kept in the app's image cache on your phone so places you've opened also work offline.

Sharing and saving your passport card

When you share a passport card, Android's share menu opens and you pick the app it goes to. When you save a card to your photos, Android asks you for permission to add pictures to your photo library. The app only adds the card; it does not read or upload your photos.

Wanderstamp Pro

Pro is an optional one-time purchase (€4.99, or the equivalent shown in your currency) that unlocks extra stamp styles, card designs, map themes and travel stats. It is bought through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment and your payment details under the Google privacy policy; we do not receive your card details. Google Play tells the app whether you own Pro, and the app remembers that on your phone. We receive the standard order reports Google Play gives developers (such as the date, country and amount of a purchase), which we use only to manage sales, refunds and taxes, and keep as long as tax law requires.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which features people use and how to improve the app, Wanderstamp sends anonymous usage statistics to Aptabase (aptabase.com), an analytics service that stores data in the EU. These are short event names with small details, for example "app opened", "page viewed: Passport", "card shared: Postcard design", "a country was stamped", or "signed in" (without who). They also include the app version, the Android version and the phone's language.

They don't include your name, email, account, the countries or places you've stamped, your location, or any device or advertising ID. Events are grouped into anonymous sessions that can't be linked to you or to each other over time, so they are not personal data.

Feedback emails

"Send feedback" in the app opens your own email app with a new message. It fills in the app version and your Android version so we can look into problems; you can remove them before sending. If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply, and delete them when they're no longer needed.

Links to other places

Some buttons open other apps or websites, such as the Play Store page to rate the app or a photo's source page on Wikimedia Commons. Those services have their own privacy policies.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No tracking or crash reporting tools, and no analytics beyond the anonymous usage statistics above.
  • No location access: you choose the places on the map yourself.
  • No access to your contacts, camera or microphone.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with anyone.

Children

Wanderstamp is not aimed at children under 13, and guests' data never leaves their phone. If you believe a child has signed in without a parent's consent, email us and we will delete the account.

Your rights

As a guest, your data is only on your phone and fully in your control. If you have signed in, you have the right under the GDPR to:

  • access the data we hold about you and get a copy of it (the app's backup file already gives you your passport in a portable format);
  • have it corrected: you can change your passport in the app at any time;
  • have it deleted: Profile → Delete account, or by email;
  • restrict or object to its use, and to data portability.

To use any of these rights, email dndtech.biz@gmail.com from the address of your account; we answer within one month. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, in Poland the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), or the authority where you live or work.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what data it collects or where it goes, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date at the top.
