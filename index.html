How it works

Tap, thunk, done.

No forms, no trip logs, no homework. Just the places you've been.

  1. Spin the globe

    Find a country you've been to. Give the globe a spin, or just search for it.

  2. Tap to stamp it

    Thunk! A stamp with the year of your trip lands in your passport.

  3. Watch your world fill up

    Country by country, your map turns orange and your countries, continents and cities add up.

Go on, give it a try

This is how it feels in the app. Tap a blank stamp and see what happens.

Questions?

Good to know

Is Wanderstamp free?

Yes! Stamping countries and cities, your wishlist, sharing your passport card and backups are all free. Pro is an optional one-time extra for keen collectors.

Do I need to make an account?

Nope. Open the app and start stamping. If you'd like your passport backed up and on more than one phone, you can sign in with Google whenever you like.

When can I get it?

Very soon. Wanderstamp is on its way to Google Play. Want a nudge when it's out? Drop us a line at dndtech.biz@gmail.com.

Is it on iPhone?

Right now Wanderstamp is made for Android phones.

What counts as a visit?

That's up to you. A two-week adventure, a weekend away or a layover you fell in love with: if it means something to you, stamp it.

A place is missing or something looks wrong?

Please tell us! Send an email, or use Profile → Send feedback in the app, and we'll put it right.
