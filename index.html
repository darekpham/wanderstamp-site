Travel tracker for Android

Your travels, stamped.

Wanderstamp is a sticker book for everywhere you've been. Tap a country on the map and a stamp lands in your passport. Keep a wishlist, add the cities you've visited, and share a passport card of your travels.

Coming soon toGoogle Play See what it does

Collect the world, one stamp at a time

No trip logs, no receipts, no sign-up needed. Just the places you've been.

🗺️

Tap the countries you've been to

Every tap drops a stamp on your map. Spin the globe, add the cities you visited and the year you first went.

📈

Watch your numbers climb

Countries, continents, regions and the share of the world you've stamped so far. Keep a wishlist of places, cities and sights for next time.

🛂

Share your passport

Post a passport card of everywhere you've been, sized for your Instagram or Facebook story, or save it to your photos.
