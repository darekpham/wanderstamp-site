Delete your Wanderstamp account

Wanderstamp by DTech Anh Pham · Last updated: 26 November 2026

A Wanderstamp account exists only if you chose Sign in with Google in the app to turn on Auto backup. If you have always used the app as a guest, there is no account and nothing about you is stored by us: uninstalling the app removes everything.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Wanderstamp and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete account again to confirm.

Your account and its cloud copy are deleted straight away. The passport on your phone stays, so you can keep using the app as a guest.

By email (without the app)

If you no longer have the app, email dndtech.biz@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Wanderstamp account. Send it from the email address of the Google account you signed in with, so we can find the account and know the request is yours. If you write from another address, tell us the account's address and we will confirm with it first.

We delete the account within 30 days of your request, usually much sooner, and reply to confirm when it's done.

What is deleted

  • your account and its user ID;
  • the email address and Google profile name stored with it;
  • the cloud copy of your passport: countries, years, cities, wishlist, notes and the name on your passport card.

This can't be undone. Copies in our provider's routine backups disappear as those backups are replaced.

What is not deleted

  • The passport on your phone. To remove it too, uninstall the app, or sign out first and choose Remove from phone.
  • Your Wanderstamp Pro purchase. It is recorded by Google Play, not by us, so it stays with your Google account; signing in or not, Pro keeps working on that account.
  • Emails you sent us, including the deletion request: we keep them only as long as needed to answer and to show the request was handled.
  • Backup files you saved yourself (for example to Google Drive): delete them wherever you saved them.
  • Free Pro. If we gave your Google account Wanderstamp Pro for free, its email address stays on our list of free Pro accounts, so Pro works again if you sign in later. Ask us by email and we remove it.

Delete some of your data and keep your account

You don't have to delete your account to remove data from it. While you are signed in, Auto backup keeps the cloud copy the same as your phone, so whatever you remove in the app is removed from the cloud copy too, within seconds when you are online:

  • A country: open it on the map and tap Visited (or Want to go) again to take it off your passport.
  • A city or a wishlist place: tap it again to take it off your passport or your wishlist.
  • A note: clear its text on the country page.
  • Your traveller name and home country: change or clear them in Profile.

To have all of your backed-up passport deleted while keeping the account, or anything else we hold about you, email dndtech.biz@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Wanderstamp data from your account's email address, and say what to delete. We do it within 30 days, usually much sooner, and reply to confirm. The account itself (its email address, Google profile name and user ID) stays until you delete the account.

More about what we store and why is in the privacy policy.
